## 2. Goal and user-observable result

A visitor can open the welcome screen, enter the static demo, and move through every section without being shown a fake save.

- `/` shows the pitch, the disclaimer, and three actions.
- **Try fictional demo** goes to `/app/demo/command-center`.
- **Create new campaign** and **Import backup** stay on `/` and say they are unavailable.
- Every campaign section, including an unknown section under a campaign id, shows `Static preview — not save data` and the disclaimer.
- Command-center KPI values are the word `Preview`. No euro amount appears.
- Squad has column headers and no player rows. Constitution says the rules are not active.
- Search and Backup explain that they are unavailable and do not open a file picker.
- `/app` and unknown URLs still show their existing titles, now with a link back to `/`.

Nothing is written to browser storage. The route table does not change.

## 6. Implementation steps

1. Add `src/app/copy.ts` with the exports in §5. Use those constants in the UI. Do not paste the same sentence a second time as a literal in a component.

2. Change `ErrorState` so `title` is an `h1`. `EmptyState` stays as it is. Missing campaign and not-found pages then keep the heading strings from `P01-G03`.

3. `WelcomePage`:
   - `PageHeader` title remains `Touchline OS`.
   - Render `pitch` and `disclaimer`.
   - **Try fictional demo** is a React Router `Link` to `/app/demo/command-center`. The visible text is `Try fictional demo`. Add `className="touchline-focus"`.
   - **Create new campaign** and **Import backup** are `Button`s. One `useState` holds `'create' | 'import' | null`.
   - The open button sets `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` to a panel id. The panel is a paragraph, not a form. Create shows `createPanel`. Import shows `importPanel`.
   - Neither button changes the URL. Do not render `<input type="file">`.

4. `WorkspaceLayout` passes `banner={previewBanner}` into `AppShell`. `AppShell` already wraps that value in `Banner`. Do not wrap it again. After `Outlet`, render `<footer>{disclaimer}</footer>` as part of the `AppShell` children, so every campaign route, including an unknown section, shows the banner and the disclaimer.

5. `TopBar` keeps the campaign id as plain text and the Settings and Help links. `Search` and `Backup` stay `Button`s. A second `useState` holds `'search' | 'backup' | null`. The open panel shows `searchPanel` or `backupPanel` and uses `aria-expanded` plus `aria-controls`. No click handler reads a file, calls `fetch`, or touches storage.

6. Each workspace feature page still renders `SectionPage` for its id, so the `h1` stays the label from `routes.ts`. Add the body under that heading.
